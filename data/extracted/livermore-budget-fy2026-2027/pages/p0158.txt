TRAFFIC IMPACT FEE

Established to account for construction of interchange and local street improvement projects identified in the City’s Traffic Impact
Fee Study. Rates are charged to developers on new construction and vary by type of project. The projected revenues are based
on how many units the City expects to be built in the upcoming years per the Engineering Department. The projected revenue
increase is due to the development of a new hotel and continued planned new development.
