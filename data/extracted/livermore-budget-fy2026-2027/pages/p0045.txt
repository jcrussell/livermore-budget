LIVERMORE-PLEASANTON FIRE DEPARTMENT – PERFORMANCE MEASURES

The Livermore-Pleasanton Fire Department (LPFD) operates five fire stations in Livermore. The
mission of LPFD is to provide exceptional public safety services that enhance the quality-of-life in the
community by protecting life, property, and the environment. Duty, Integrity, Professionalism, Courage
and Honor are the LPFD’s core values. Demand for emergency response continues to grow, including
response to California wildfires.
