EDUCATIONAL REVENUE AUGMENTATION FUND (ERAF) LOSS HISTORY (PROPERTY TAX)

ERAF is the mechanism by which the State of California uses local entity property tax revenue to meet
the State's statutory requirement to fund education. The City is anticipating additional ERAF losses of
between $15.2 million and $15.9 million in each of the fiscal years covered in the current Financial Plan.
The cumulative loss of property tax revenue to the City of Livermore since the inception of ERAF in fiscal
year 1992-93 is a staggering $217.7 million through FY 2023-24 with additional ERAF losses of the
former Redevelopment Agency totaling an additional $2.7 million.
