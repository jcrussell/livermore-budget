BUSINESS LICENSE TAX The recession had a negative impact on business license tax revenue beginning in fiscal year 2008-09 which lasted five years. Additionally, fiscal year 2011-12 was impacted by the loss of one of the City's traditionally larger business license tax generators which moved outside of the City. Business license revenues began to recover beginning in FY 2012-13 but then declined in FY 2019-20 due to the shelter-in-place directive in response to the COVID-19 pandemic. Revenue growth beginning in FY 2020-21 reflects a gradual recovery from the pandemic business restrictions. The jump in revenue starting with FY 2022-23 is due to the addition of business license tax from the two national labs. Business license revenue projected for FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27 is flat, reflecting the offset between tax increase generated by new businesses and tax decrease as existing busineses continue to grapple with rising business expenses.
