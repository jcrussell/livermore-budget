AIRPORT REVENUES

Airport Rent revenues are primarily comprised of short and medium term hangar and tie-down space rents as well as
Fixed Base Operator (FBO) ground rent. The Airport leases 393 hangar units and has approximately 90 aircraft tie-down
spaces available. Approximately 465 aircraft are currently based at the Airport and all hangar units are occupied. Due to
demand for hangar space, staff maintains a hangar waiting list. When the full-service FBO took over fuel sales in mid FY
2015-16, the Airport started collecting Fuel Flowage Fees.
