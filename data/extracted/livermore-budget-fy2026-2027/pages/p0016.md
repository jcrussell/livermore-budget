**GFOA DISTINGUISHED BUDGET PRESENTATION AWARD**

The Government Finance Officers Association of the United States and Canada (GFOA) presented a Distinguished Budget Presentation Award to City of Livermore, California for its biennial budget Fiscal Years beginning July 1, 20 23. To receive this award, a governmental unit must publish a budget document that meets program criteria as a policy document, as an operations guide, as a financial plan, and as a communications device. This award is valid for a period of two years only.
