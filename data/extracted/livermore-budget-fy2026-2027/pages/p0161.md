LOW INCOME HOUSING FEE Low Income Housing fee is collected from developers during construction of residential and commercial projects. The fee provides for land acquisition, construction and acquisition of residential units, which are affordable to low-income households. Fees vary based on type of project that is being constructed. Annual growth rates are subject to the cyclical nature of development and the economy.
