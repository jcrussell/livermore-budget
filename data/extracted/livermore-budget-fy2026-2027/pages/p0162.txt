                                                     REVENUES


RENTAL OF CITY OWNED PROPERTY

Over 75% of the revenue comes from subterranean property use. The General Fund is charging the utilities (Water, Sewer &
Stormwater) a rent for the cost of the City of Livermore's right of way (ROW) applicable to the City's underground utilities based
upon a study done in July 2009 by MGT of American, Inc. The rents are adjusted annually by CPI. The remaining revenue
comes from monthly rental income from commercial properties that the City owns, primarily Las Positas Golf Course. The golf
course lease began during FY 2018-19.
