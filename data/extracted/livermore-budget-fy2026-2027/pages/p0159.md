PARK FEE – AB 1600 Established to account for acquisition and construction of trail and park facilities necessary to accommodate new development. Projects are included in the City's General Plan, City and Livermore Area Recreation and Park District (LARPD) Trails Master Plan, and LARPD's Park Master Plan. The revenue is generated from fees charged to developers. Fees vary based on type of project that is being constructed. Annual growth rates are subject to the cyclical nature of development and the economy.
