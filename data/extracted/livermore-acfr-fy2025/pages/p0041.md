Management's Discussion and Analysis June 30, 2025

The General Fund revenues were $157.2 million in FY 2024-25, an $11.7 million increase from FY 2023-24 amount of $145.5 million. As discussed in detail under "The City's Fund Financial Statements", increase mainly came from property tax ($2.72 million), use of money and property ($2.41 million), licenses and permits ($4.38 million) and other taxes ($2.97 million). Decreases mainly came from charges for current services ($3.15 million) from the prior year.
