Note 4 -    Other Post-Employment Benefits

Schedule of Changes in the City’s Net OPEB Liability and Related Ratios

This schedule presents information about the components of the net OPEB liability and related ratios, including the
OPEB plan’s fiduciary net position as a percentage of the total OPEB liability, and the net OPEB liability as a
percentage of covered-employee payroll.

Schedule of Contributions

This schedule reports the City’s contributions to the plan which are actuarially determined, the employer’s actual
contributions, the difference between the actual and actuarially determined contributions, and a ratio of the actual
contributions divided by covered-employee payroll.
