Interest Rate Risk

Interest rate risk represents how changes in market interest rates will adversely affect the fair value of an
investment. Normally, the longer the maturity of an investment, the greater the sensitivity of its fair value to
changes in market interest rates. The City generally manages its interest rate risk by holding investments to
maturity.
