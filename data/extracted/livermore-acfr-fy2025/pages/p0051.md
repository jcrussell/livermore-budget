Government Fund Statements June 30, 2025

**Fund Financial Statements**

Only individual major funds are presented in the Fund Financial Statements, while non-major funds are combined in a single column. Major funds are defined generally as having significant activities or balances in the current year. The City has identified the funds below as major governmental funds:

**General Fund**

The General Fund is used for all the general revenues of the City not specifically levied or collected for other City funds and related expenditures.

**Low Income Housing Special Revenue Fund**

Established to account for the receipt and disbursement of in-lieu low-income housing impact fees collected from developers.
