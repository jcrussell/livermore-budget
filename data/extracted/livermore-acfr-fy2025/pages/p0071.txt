                                                                                                   City of Livermore
                                                                                          Notes to Financial Statements
                                                                                                          June 30, 2025


The City invests in individual investments and in investment pools. Individual investments are evidenced by specific
identifiable securities instruments, or by an electronic entry registering the owner in the records of the institution
issuing the security, called the book entry system. In order to increase security, the City employs the Trust
Department of a bank as the custodian of certain City managed investments, regardless of their form.

The City’s investments are carried at fair value, as required by generally accepted accounting principles. The City
adjusts the carrying value of its investments to reflect their fair value at each fiscal year end, and it includes the
effects of these adjustments in income for that fiscal year.
