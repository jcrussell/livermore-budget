Note 19 - Restatement due to Change in Accounting Principle

As of June 30, 2025, the City adopted GASB Statement No. 101, Compensated Absences. The provisions of this
standard modernize the types of leave that are considered a compensated absence and provide guidance for a
consistent recognition and measurement of the compensated absence liability. Therefore, compensated absences
for the governmental activities and business-type activities were increased by $1,325,287 and $215,045,
respectively, as of July 1, 2024. The effect of this change in accounting principle is described in the table below.
