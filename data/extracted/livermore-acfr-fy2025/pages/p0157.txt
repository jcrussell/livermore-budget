Custodial Funds

Custodial funds are used to account for assets held by the City for other entities and individuals. Such funds are
operated to carry out the specific actions of trust agreements, ordinances, and other governing regulations.

Community Facilities and Assessment Districts’ Bonds
This fund accounts for the collection of property owners' debt service obligation which is used to pay principal and
interest on 1915 Act Bonds.

Other Custodial Funds
This fund accounts for assets which are held for other governmental agencies or individuals by the City in a custodial
capacity.
