**Government-wide Financial Analysis**

As noted earlier, the City's net position serves as a useful indicator of whether the City's financial position is improving or deteriorating. Other factors, such as market conditions, should be considered when measuring the City's overall financial position. This analysis focuses on the net position and changes in net position of the City as a whole. As shown in the chart below, total government-wide assets and deferred outflows of resources exceeded liabilities and deferred inflows of resources by $793.1 million, at the close of FY 2024-25.

Long‐term liabilities, which consists mostly of claims, debt, compensated absences, pension, and postemployment benefit obligations, decreased by $16.7 million from the previous year for governmental and business‐type activities combined. The City's debt decreased by $7.0 million due to scheduled debt payments. The City's total pension liability increased by $7.8 million.
