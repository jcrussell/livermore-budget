Management's Discussion and Analysis June 30, 2025

The General Fund budget for FY 2025-26 provides $158.4 million in resources, and $158.4 million in uses, including $2.2 million for the Capital Improvement Plan (CIP). The FY 2025-26 budget includes a General Fund budget surplus of $0.01 million. The General Fund's FY 2025-26 operating expenditures are projected to grow by $3.6 million to $144.7 million from FY 2024-25 budgeted expenditures of $141.1 million primarily related to increases in employment-related costs. The fund balance for the General Fund at the end of FY 2024-25 is $87.0 million with $44.3 million in reserves and $1.2 million in spendable fund balance available for appropriations, excluding capital reserves.
