**Note 12 - Other Post Employment Benefits**

The City has two types of retirement health benefits as determined by Council resolution and through agreements with its labor units. One plan is a Defined Benefit Plan (Other Post Employment Benefit "OPEB") which provides health care benefits for retired employees under third party insurance plans. The second plan is a Defined Contribution Plan in which the City contributes a fixed amount to a retirement health savings account on behalf of employees who are not eligible for OPEB. The City's Defined Benefit OPEB plan is closed to new entrants; all new employees are enrolled into the City's Defined Contribution plan.
