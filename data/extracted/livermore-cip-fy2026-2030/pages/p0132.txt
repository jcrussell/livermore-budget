     Project Description:
     Install advance stop bar, LPI, flashing beacons,
     and advance warning at signalized intersections;
     install RRFB, larger/additional warning/regulatory
     signs at unsignalized intersections and install
     speed feedback signs on major corridors.




     Project Justification:
     The city was recently selected for a total of $2.25 Millions of Cycle 12 Highway Safety Improvement Program
     (HSIP) funding, with 10 percent local match. All four applications were selected. These projects were
     recommended as part of the Local Roadway Safety Plan (LRSP).
