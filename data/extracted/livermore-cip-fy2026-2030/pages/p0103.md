## Project Description:

Annual repairs to existing City sidewalks including curb/gutter and street trees. This project pertains to sidewalks city-wide which are predominantly city maintained including decorative sidewalks in the Downtown Specific Plan (SDTSP) area. This project involves slicing, remove and repair phases and is scheduled to be completed in FY 2024-25.

## Project Justification:

This project will remove potential tripping hazards on backing lots and sidewalks adjacent to City properties, making the sidewalks safer for pedestrians.
