## ARROYO ROAD PATH

Project No.: TB201723

**PROJECT LOCATION**

East of Arroyo Road from Latour Avenue to Hanson Road

## Project Description:

Several segments and portions of the existing public path/sidewalk along the east side of Arroyo Road, from Latour Avenue to Hansen Road, were constructed inside seven abutting private parcels as part of Tract 7071. This project identified and acquired the needed easement rights for encroaching areas on private property and existing agricultural conservation easement requiring a right of way agent, legal counsel, and coordination with the Tri-Valley Conservancy. The City is working on acquiring sidewalk easements from two property owners.

## Project Justification:

This right of way acquisition effort will correct and secure easement rights for the segments of public trail currently encroaching into private property along Arroyo Road.
