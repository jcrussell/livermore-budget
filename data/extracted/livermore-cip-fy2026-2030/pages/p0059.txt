                                                     PROJECT LOCATION

     Project Description:                                City Hall

     Install two solar-powered electrical vehicle
     charging stations in City employee parking lots.
     These portable units are stand alone and don't
     require any utilities or site modifications. Each
     unit provides up to 4.3KW and can be connected
     to two vehicles. Each unit has a 32 KW-hour
     battery. Project also includes installing two Tesla
     chargers in the Police Building employee parking
     lot. Additionally, the budget covers preliminary
     design and grant application costs for the Phase 2
     Fleet Electrification Project. This work was
     completed in FY 2024-25.

     Project Justification:
     The City is going to start replacing vehicles at the end of their service lives with electric vehicles. The two solar-
     powered electrical vehicle charging stations will be able to charge up to four electric vehicles. The City can use
     these to evaluate the effectiveness of an electric vehicle fleet. Vehicle emissions will be reduced due to the solar
     charging. The 32 KW-hour batteries can be used for charging at nighttime or for other electrical demands during
     a power outage.
