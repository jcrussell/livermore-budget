## ALTAMONT TANK RECOATING

Project No.: WT202412

**PROJECT LOCATION**

Patterson Pass Road

## Project Description:

The Altamont tank is a 5-million-gallon potable water steel storage tank constructed in 2003. The interior of the tank will be recoated, and the gaskets/rubber boots will be replaced.

## Project Justification:

The tank was inspected and cleaned in December 2022. The interior roof coating is deteriorating, exposing bare steel, which is beginning to corrode. The gaskets and rubber boots are deteriorating and losing elasticity. Recoating and replacing the gaskets and rubber boots will help ensure the integrity of the water storage tank and help maintain critical water supplies.
