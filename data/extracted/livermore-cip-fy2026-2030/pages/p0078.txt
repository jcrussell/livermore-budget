                                                     PROJECT LOCATION
                                                       Heather Lane
     Project Description:
     This project includes design and construction of
     mitigation to offset the impacts of maintenance
     work done along Altamont Creek.




     Project Justification:
     Altamont Creek was desilted without permits. In lieu of levying violations, environmental resource agencies
     agreed to off-site mitigation equal to what permits would have required. The city is looking at locations for new
     riparian areas. The best location appears to be on the former Springtown Golf Course downstream of Heather
     Lane and south of the creek. City staff cleared sediment from this section of Altamont Creek and now must work
     with Zone 7 to restore the creek to mitigate for impacts from clearing the sediment.
