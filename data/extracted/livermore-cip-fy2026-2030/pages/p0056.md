## Project Description:

This project will provide a new elastomeric coating on the Exterior Insulation Finishing Systems (EIFS) on the Civic Center Library. City Hall and the Police Department were recoated as part of the Recoating of Building Exterior Insulation Finishing System (EIFS) Project in FY 2015-16.

## Project Justification:

Recoating needs to be completed to keep the warranty valid. Additionally, recoating prevents further damage to the exterior and extends the life of the Library exterior.
