     Project Description:
     Annual project to replace/modify and maintain
     signing, striping, pavement markings, and
     associated infrastructure as needed.




     Project Justification:
     As traffic conditions and MUTCD standards change, existing signing, striping pavement markings, and related
     infrastructure require modifications. This project would allow the city to continually improve traffic safety by
     maintaining, replacing, and upgrading existing signing/striping/pavement markings as needed.
