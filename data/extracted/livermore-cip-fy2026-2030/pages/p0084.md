**PROJECT LOCATION**

Citywide

## Project Description:

The project will install new storm drains to reduce ponding areas during heavy rains; this may involve minor grading. High priority locations include First Street at Old First, various locations along Greenville Road, Southfront Road, 1901 College Avenue, 636 Old Oak Road, 713 McLeod Street, and 2168 Normandy Circle. The City will continue to look for other priority locations susceptible to heavy storm events.

## Project Justification:

Ponding could pose a safety hazard to vehicles and pedestrians as well as a vector concern if not drained within 48 hours. The project will resolve drainage concerns by reducing ponding that occurs during heavy rain events.
