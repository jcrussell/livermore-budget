     Project Description:
     This project involves installing a new portion of the
     Montage Trail to close the gap and connect the trail
     to Collier Canyon Road. Construction of the new
     portion of the trail was completed in FY 2024-25.




     Project Justification:
     This project will close a gap in the trail system identified in the Active Transportation Plan.
