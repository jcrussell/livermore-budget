## Project Description:

This project will implement roadway improvements in the vicinity of East Avenue as recommended by the approved 2018 ATP and East Avenue Corridor Study. The project includes modifying elements from Tactical East Avenue (bulb outs and medians) to a more permanent configuration and adding bulb outs to up to three additional intersections. Improvements also include developing a bicycle boulevard through the neighborhood north of East Avenue (ATP Projects BB24, BB26, and BB37).
