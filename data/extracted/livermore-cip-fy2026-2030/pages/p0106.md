## Project Description:

This project involves annual repairs to existing City sidewalks including curb/gutter and street trees. This project pertains to sidewalks citywide which are predominantly City maintained including decorative sidewalks in the DTSP area. The project involves slicing, remove, and repair phases.

## Project Justification:

This project will remove potential tripping hazards on backing lots and sidewalks adjacent to City properties, making the sidewalks safer for pedestrians.
