     Project Description:
     In 2017, the City of Livermore experienced storm damage in
     multiple locations. All projects within this project have been
     completed, except for the Cottonwood Creek Water Line and
     Bank Erosion Restoration Project (FEMA PA-09-CA-4308-PW-
     0097, LOP#19). The storm caused erosion to the banks and creek
     bed of Cottonwood Creek exposing a major transmission
     domestic waterline and reducing the cover over a reclaimed
     waterline to less than one foot. Additionally, the storm damaged
     riparian mitigation planting areas that were established under the
     El Charro Mitigation Plan. Temporary repairs were made to
     protect the domestic waterline, the reclaimed waterline, and the
     banks. However, further work is necessary to permanently
     reinforce the utilities, stabilize the banks and creek, and restore
     riparian mitigation planting areas. This project will incorporate
     floodplain benches, pools, riffles, and hardening elements.
     Project Justification:
     The project focuses on enhancing scour and flood protection for the channel and utilities, restoring the riparian
     planting areas, and creating a more stable reach of Cottonwood Creek downstream of the I-580 interchange.
     Coordination with regulatory agencies on this project has been lengthy. The agencies required the City of
     Livermore to develop a comprehensive design solution that includes the upstream reaches of the damaged site.
     This condition aims to mitigate future failure.
