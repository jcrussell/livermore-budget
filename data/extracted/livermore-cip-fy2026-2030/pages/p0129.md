## CROSSWALK SAFETY IMPROVEMENTS

Project No.: TC202506

**PROJECT LOCATION**

Multiple locations citywide

## Project Description:

This project will design and install crosswalk safety improvements at various locations in the City. The improvements may include (but not limited to) high visibility crosswalk, pedestrian activated rectangular rapid flashing beacon, electronic messaging, lighting, signing, and striping. Priorities to be determined after studying various crossings.
