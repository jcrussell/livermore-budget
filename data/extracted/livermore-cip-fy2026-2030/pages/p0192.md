project. Airway Boulevard at I-580

## Project Description:

The City's Airway Lift Station is a booster pump station that boosts potable water received from Zone 7 Water Agency's distribution system to the Doolan Tank water storage reservoir that serves the City's Zone 1 Water System. The City will improve the existing pump station to meet the operational requirements for efficiencies and capacities and reduce maintenance costs. The improvements are anticipated to include replacement of pumps, emergency generator, and electrical components. The station improvements will comply with the codes for mechanical, electrical, structural, control, instrumentation, and reliability.

## Project Justification:

Over recent years, the pressure in the Zone 7 distribution system has dropped, significantly impacting pump station performance. It is also anticipated that the Zone 7 distribution system pressure will decrease further with projected service area demand, requiring not only near-term changes to the pump station to address current performance issues, but also upgrades that consider projected long-term conditions in the system. The improved water pump station will supply the distribution system with water at adequate flows and pressures. The improvements will bring the system up to current code requirements, improve operational controls and reliability, and reduce maintenance costs.
