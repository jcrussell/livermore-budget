     Project Description:
     Connection of North Canyons Parkway and Dublin Boulevard as a 4 lane major roadway between Doolan Canyon
     Road and the east Dublin City limits. The project will have a bridge crossing over Cottonwood Creek within the
     jurisdiction of Alameda County and will require coordination with Alameda County. City of Dublin is the lead
     agency. This project includes improvements to North Canyons Parkway between Doolan Road and Airway
     Boulevard. Project funding is for City coordination and improvements within City limits.
     Project Justification:
     The project will provide transportation alternatives in the I-580 corridor and relieve congestion.
