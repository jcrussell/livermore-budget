## Project Description:

This project will install a gate at the Civic Center Library at the entrance to the employee parking lot.

## Project Justification:

The employee lot at the Civic Center Library is semisecluded and hard to monitor. Installing a gate will keep public vehicles out of this area which will increase security for employees and reduce maintenance associated with debris removal and other activities.
