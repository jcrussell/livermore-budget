## Project Justification:

There are several Arroyo Las Positas flood mitigation projects planned at the golf course location to protect the Livermore Municipal Airport and other infrastructure. The Golf Course Damage Repairs Project \# 202132 is one of the projects. The other projects are: Arroyo Las Positas Desilting Through Las Positas Golf Course, Project \# 202015, Las Positas Golf Course Clubhouse Parking Lot Drainage Improvements, Project \# 202525 and the Airway Culvert Replacement Project \# 202616. These projects offer comprehensive flood mitigation measures and aim to protect Livermore Municipal Airport and other critical infrastructures.
