## Project Description:

The 2023 Winter Storm Event caused damage to numerous locations in the City, varying from minor to major. The restoration work is separated into several categories: debris removal (CAT A), emergency protective measures or emergency opening (CAT B), permanent repairs (CAT C-G), and general, administrative, and other miscellaneous meetings (CAT Z). This project is for Category C- G permanent repairs. As of FY 2024-25, the majority of the permanent repairs have been completed. The slope slide repair at Portola Avenue is the only site that remains, and this work will consist of rebuilding/recompacting the slope, concrete drainage installation, and slope underdrain installation, and is estimated to be completed in FY 2026-27. The City has applied for federal and state funding.

## Project Justification:

The sites must be restored for public safety and for public use of the facilities. For the remaining slope slide repair at Portola Avenue, the design and environmental coordination is currently underway, and construction is estimated to occur in FY 2026-27.
