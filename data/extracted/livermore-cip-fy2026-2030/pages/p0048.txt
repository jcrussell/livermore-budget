     Project Description:                             Caminito and

     This project will replace approximately 2,000
     linear feet of wood stake fence on the west
     side of Holmes Street between El Caminito and
     Mocho Street. The new fence will be
     constructed of wood, 7-ft tall, and on the
     property line. The fence will be painted to
     match the fence that was replaced on the east
     side of Holmes Street south of Concannon
     Boulevard. This will be the third phase of fence
     replacement along Holmes Street.




     Project Justification:
     Replacing the fence along Holmes Street will beautify a major gateway into the City. The existing fence is
     unsightly, deteriorated, and patched in many locations. The new fence will also increase safety along the
     sidewalk.
