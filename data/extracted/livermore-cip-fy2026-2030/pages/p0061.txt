     Project Description:
     This project will recoat Exterior Insulation and
     Finish Systems (EIFS) on all nine buildings at
     the Maintenance Service Center and the wall
     around the facility. The roll-up doors, man-
     doors, and trim of each building will be
     painted. The walls surrounding the yard and all
     arbors within the confines of the Maintenance
     Service Center will also be painted.




     Project Justification:
     This is the first line of protection for preserving the exterior of the buildings. The Maintenance Service Center
     buildings and infrastructure have not been painted in over 20 years.
